Marine acoustic intelligence

Hear what the ocean is telling you.

Aazhiyam AI classifies underwater vessel noise, visualizes acoustic impact across shipping corridors, and helps port authorities and conservation teams protect marine life.

Why it matters

Underwater noise is a growing threat to marine life

Whales, dolphins, and fish rely on sound to navigate, feed, and communicate. Shipping noise can mask those signals across kilometers of ocean.

◐

Acoustic classification

Hydrophone audio is scored against vessel-noise signatures to return a precise frequency reading and severity status.

How it works

A spectrogram is extracted from the clip and scored against known vessel-noise signatures, returning a frequency in Hz and a Normal/Elevated/Extreme severity label — never a color alone.

◭

Digital twin visualization

Vessels and their acoustic footprints render on a live corridor scene, showing exactly where impact concentrates.

How it works

Each tracked vessel is rendered with concentric acoustic rings that pulse in real time, colored by severity so hotspots in the corridor are visible at a glance.

▤

Compliance reporting

Authorized officials generate audit-ready reports combining readings, visuals, and recommendations.

How it works

A signed-in official can compile the current analysis, vessel data, and a digital twin snapshot into a printable report in one click.

How it works

From hydrophone audio to a protected quiet path

  1. Capture. A hydrophone records underwater sound in a monitored corridor.
  2. Classify. An on-device model scores the clip and returns a frequency and severity level.
  3. Visualize. A digital twin shows vessels and their acoustic footprint in real time.
  4. Advise. An AI copilot suggests speed and route adjustments to reduce impact.
  5. Report. Authorized officials generate a compliance snapshot for records or regulators.
